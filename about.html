Our Story

Where clay meets creativity.

RANG is a Jaipur-based pottery and art studio created for people who want to slow down, get creative and experience the simple joy of making something by hand.

About RANG

A little studio with a lot of heart.

Inspired by Jaipur's rich artistic culture, RANG brings together traditional pottery techniques and a modern creative experience.

Whether you are picking up clay for the first time or already love ceramics, our studio is a welcoming space to learn, experiment and create.

We believe that handmade objects don't need to be perfect. They just need to have a story.
